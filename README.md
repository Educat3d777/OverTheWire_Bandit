## Writeup Format

Each writeup contains:

- **Objective**: what the level asks for
- **Commands Used**: the commands I tried
- **Methodology**: what I did, what happened, and why
- **Challenges Encountered**: where I got stuck or was wrong
- **Key Takeaways**: what I learned
- **Result and References**
